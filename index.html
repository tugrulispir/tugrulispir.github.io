Efsane Futbol

A mobile football management game. You pick a club, or build a squad from scratch, then handle transfers, tactics and match-day decisions across a full season.

The game is built with AI-assisted development. My role is everything around the code: deciding what to build, designing how each system works, balancing the numbers, and playtesting every build to find what breaks.

  • 3 game modesCareer, Tournament and Friendly
  • Full seasonFixtures, league table, top scorers
  • Live matchesText commentary with in-match tactics

Transfers that feel like a negotiation

Every club has a hidden minimum price and limited patience. You bid, they counter, and after three rejected offers they leave the table. You can accept their asking price at any time, but it eats into your budget.

AI clubs buy and sell from each other on their own budgets, and their deals appear as breaking news in the career hub, so the world keeps moving without you.

Why: a plain "buy" button makes transfers a shopping list. Hidden limits and patience turn each one into a small risk-and-reward decision.

Match day with room to step in

Matches play out as live commentary at 0.5x, 1x or 2x speed, with player fitness dropping as the minutes pass. At any moment you can pause to change mentality, from parking the bus to a full-pitch press, switch formation or make substitutions.

Touchline instructions like "push forward" or "wake up" boost the team for a while, then go on a 15-minute match cooldown.

Why: without a cooldown players tap every boost all the time. The limit makes timing the decision.

Goals beyond the next match

Tiered achievements track lifetime numbers, like total wins and goals, and seasonal records, like goals in one season or the longest winning streak. Each tier shows progress toward the next one.

Why: a management game lives on the "one more match" feeling. Visible progress toward the next tier gives players a reason to come back after the season's big moments.

A small economy that connects the modes

Quick friendly matches earn coins. Coins unlock bigger tournaments: the 4-team knockout is free, the 8-team knockout costs 500 and the 16-team group format costs 1,000.

Why: it gives the short mode a purpose and paces access to the longer ones.

How I work on it

I write down how a feature should behave, build it with AI tools, then play it until something feels wrong. Most of my time goes into that last part: finding bugs in the match engine or transfer logic, deciding which ones matter, and tuning numbers like transfer prices and team ratings.
